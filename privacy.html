Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Shooter Ready does not collect your data, does not store it on any server and does not share it with anyone. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. The app makes no network requests.

Training data (runs, shot times, sessions, plans, firearm profile, settings) is stored only on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Apple Health. With your permission the Apple Watch app saves a session as a workout in Health and reads heart rate for the time of that session; the iPhone app reads that heart rate to show it next to your runs. If you delete a session in the app, its workout is removed from Health. Health data never leaves your devices and is never used for advertising.

Bluetooth is used only to connect to an SG Timer and receive shot times.

Deleting your data. Delete sessions in the Journal, or delete the app from your iPhone and Apple Watch to remove everything.

Support

The SG Timer does not connect

Turn the timer on, keep it within a few meters and allow Bluetooth for Shooter Ready in iPhone Settings. During a session the phone keeps the connection and reconnects by itself; tap the timer badge on the session card to retry.

No heart rate

Wear the watch, start the session and allow Health access: iPhone → Health → profile → Apps → Shooter Ready.

Delete a session

Journal → “…” next to the session → Delete. Its workout is removed from Health too.

No SG Timer?

Choose “Phone timer” or “Manual entry” in Settings → Time source.

Questions and bug reports: GitHub Issues
